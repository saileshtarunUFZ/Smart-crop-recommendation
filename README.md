## ✨ Key Features
* 🌾 **AI Crop Recommendation:** Predicts the best crop to plant based on your soil health (N-P-K), temperature, and rainfall.
* 💧 **Water Management:** Tells you exactly how much water your chosen crop needs and suggests smart irrigation tips.
* 🐛 **Pesticide Guide:** Recommends the safest and most effective pest control methods to protect your harvest.
* 💰 **Live Mandi Prices:** Displays current crop market prices to help you sell at the best time and make more profit.
* 🏛️ **Government Schemes:** Lists active farming subsidies, financial aids, and insurance programs you can apply for.

---

## 📋 How It Works
1. **Test Soil & Environment:** Enter your soil nutrients (N, P, K), pH level, and local weather details.
2. **Get AI Choice:** The app instantly names the most profitable crop for your land.
3. **Get Full Guide:** Along with the crop, you get instant data on required water, pesticide safety steps, current market prices, and matching government benefits.
